Are Grocery Loyalty Programs Worth It? How Store Apps Change What You Pay

More and more of the best grocery prices are behind a free account. Here's how loyalty programs work, what you trade for the discounts, and how to use them without overspending.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

For stores you shop at regularly, grocery loyalty programs are usually worth it. They're typically free, and they unlock member prices, digital coupons and rewards. In exchange, the store collects data about what you buy. Use them for the savings, but keep comparing prices. A member price isn't always the lowest price nearby.

What you give in return

The discounts aren't free. Loyalty programs let stores see what you buy, when and how often. They use that to personalize offers, plan inventory and pricing, and in some cases share or sell insights to brands and advertisers, depending on the program's privacy policy.

  • Read the privacy settings in the app and opt out of what you can.
  • Limit app permissions, such as location, to what you're comfortable with.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails if they tempt you to buy more.

How to use loyalty programs well

  1. Join the programs of the 2–3 stores you actually use.
  2. Clip digital coupons for items already on your list, before you shop.
  3. Scan your account at the start of checkout.
  4. Ignore offers for things you don't need.
  5. Compare member prices with other stores for your top items.

FAQ

Are grocery loyalty programs worth it?

For stores you shop at regularly, usually yes. They're typically free and unlock member prices, digital coupons and rewards. Keep comparing prices with other stores.

What do grocery stores do with loyalty data?

They use it to personalize offers and plan pricing and inventory, and depending on the program's privacy policy, may share insights with brands and advertisers.

Can I get member prices without an account?

Usually not. Member prices generally require a loyalty account, though it's typically free.

Do loyalty programs make you spend more?

They can, through personalized offers and rewards that encourage loyalty. Stick to your list and compare prices.

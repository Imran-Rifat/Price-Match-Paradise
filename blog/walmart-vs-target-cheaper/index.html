Walmart vs. Target: Which Is Cheaper for Groceries?

Every comparison article gives you a national answer. Your bill is local. Here's how to find out which one is actually cheaper for the things you buy, where you live.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

There's no single answer, because both chains price by area and neither is cheapest on everything. Walmart is widely known for everyday low prices, while Target leans on sales and its Circle deals. The reliable way to know is a basket test: compare the unit price of the 15–20 items you buy most at both, at your local stores.

Why national comparisons don't settle it

  • Prices vary by location. Both chains price by area based on local costs and competition. See how grocery pricing works.
  • Different strengths. One may win on pantry staples and the other on household goods or seasonal items.
  • Sales and member prices move week to week, which changes the answer.
  • Store brands differ. Comparing name brands only tells you part of the story.

The basket test (about 15 minutes)

  1. Write down your top 15–20 items, the ones you buy nearly every week.
  2. Scan each item in one store to capture prices across stores, including the other chain.
  3. Compare unit prices, not package prices, since sizes differ.
  4. Tally the winners. You'll usually find each store wins a group of items.
  5. Decide your split: one main store plus a top-up trip, or switch entirely.

FAQ

Is Walmart cheaper than Target?

It depends on the items and your area. Both price by location, and each tends to win on different categories. Compare the unit prices of the items you buy most.

Does Target price match Walmart?

No. Target stopped matching competitors in July 2025 and only matches its own prices.

Does Walmart price match Target?

No. Walmart only matches Walmart.com prices in its US stores.

How do I compare grocery prices between stores?

Use unit prices for the 15–20 items you buy most, and compare them at your local stores. A barcode scanner app does this in seconds per item.

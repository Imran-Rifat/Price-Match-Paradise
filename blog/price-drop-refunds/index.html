Price Dropped After You Bought It? Stores That Refund the Difference (2026)

Price adjustments are one of the easiest ways to get money back, but windows and rules vary a lot. Here's who offers them, how long you have, and how to claim.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

Several big retailers refund the difference if their own price drops after you buy: Target within 14 days, Costco within 30 days, Best Buy within its return window (15 days for most products for most customers), and Home Depot typically within 30 days. Walmart doesn't offer price adjustments. Bring your receipt and ask at customer service, or contact the store for online orders.

How to claim a price adjustment

  1. Find the new lower price for the identical item at the same store (or its website, if the policy allows).
  2. Check you're inside the window and that the item isn't excluded, such as clearance or a limited-time event.
  3. Bring your receipt (or order number) to customer service, or use chat or phone for online orders.
  4. Get the difference refunded to your original payment method.

What about "return and rebuy"?

If a store won't adjust but you're still within the return window, some shoppers return the item and buy it again at the lower price. It can work, but stores can refuse, and it's not an option for opened or final-sale items. Check the store's return policy first.

FAQ

Which stores refund the difference if a price drops?

Target (14 days), Costco (30 days), Best Buy (15 days for most products, longer for some members) and Home Depot (typically 30 days) refund their own price drops. Walmart doesn't.

Does Walmart do price adjustments?

No. Walmart's published policy doesn't offer price adjustments after purchase, online or in store.

Do I need my receipt for a price adjustment?

Usually, or an order number for online purchases. Costco can often find warehouse purchases through your membership.

What is the difference between a price adjustment and a price match?

A price adjustment refunds the difference when the same store lowers its own price after you buy. A price match lowers the price to match a competitor's lower price.

Coupon Stacking Explained: How to Combine Discounts the Right Way

Stacking means using more than one discount on the same item. Done right, it's the difference between a good price and a great one. Here's how the pieces fit together.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

Coupon stacking means combining discounts on one item. Many stores allow one manufacturer coupon plus one store coupon per item, on top of the sale price, and you can often add a cash-back or rebate app after you buy. Rules vary by store, so check its coupon policy. And compare the final price with other stores. A stacked deal isn't always the lowest price.

The types of discounts you can combine

Sale priceThe store
Manufacturer coupon (paper or digital)The brand. The store is reimbursed
Store coupon / digital offerThe store
Loyalty rewardsThe store
Cash-back or rebate appThe app or brand, after purchase
Cash-back credit cardThe card issuer

Because manufacturer coupons and store coupons are paid for by different companies, many stores let you use one of each on the same item. Two manufacturer coupons on one item generally isn't allowed.

Rules to check in your store's coupon policy

  • How many manufacturer and store coupons per item.
  • Whether coupons can be used on sale items, BOGO items or price-matched items.
  • Limits on identical coupons per transaction.
  • Whether a coupon can exceed the item's price (usually no "overage").
  • Whether digital coupons must be clipped before checkout (usually yes).

Coupon policies are usually posted on the store's website. Search "[store name] coupon policy."

Stacking tips that save time

  • Start with the sale. Coupons are most powerful on items that are already discounted.
  • Clip digital coupons in the store app before you shop.
  • Only stack on things you'd buy anyway. A great deal on something you don't need is still money spent.

FAQ

What is coupon stacking?

Using more than one discount on the same item, such as a sale price, a manufacturer coupon and a store coupon together.

Can I use a manufacturer coupon and a store coupon on the same item?

Many stores allow one of each per item, since they're funded by different companies. Check your store's coupon policy.

Can I use two manufacturer coupons on one item?

Generally no. Most coupon policies allow one manufacturer coupon per item.

Can I use coupons with a price match?

It depends on the store. Some allow it, some don't. Check the price match and coupon policies.

Costco vs. Sam's Club: Which One Actually Saves You More?

The honest answer depends on your basket, your area and which club is closer. Here's a simple method to work it out for your household in one shopping trip.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

Neither club is cheaper for everyone. It depends on the items you buy, the prices at your local clubs and the membership fee. Compare the unit price of your top 15 items at both, subtract the annual fee, and check whether a regular supermarket beats both on some items. Costco's Gold Star membership is $65 a year ($130 for Executive). Check current Sam's Club fees, which changed recently.

The 15-item method

  1. List the 15 items your household buys most in bulk.
  2. Record the unit price for each at both clubs (per ounce, count or load), not the pack price.
  3. Add your regular supermarket for the same items. Sometimes it wins on sale.
  4. Add up the annual difference based on how often you buy each item.
  5. Subtract the membership fee and see what's left.
Break-even: membership fee ÷ savings per trip = trips needed per year. A $65 fee with $10 saved per trip needs about 7 trips a year to pay off. More in is buying in bulk worth it.

Tips either way

  • Try before committing. Shop with a friend's membership or a guest option where available.
  • Watch the impulse buys. Clubs are designed for big baskets.
  • Split bulk items with another household.
  • Reassess yearly at renewal, using your actual savings.

FAQ

Is Costco or Sam's Club cheaper?

It depends on your basket and local prices. Compare the unit price of the 15 items you buy most at both, then subtract the membership fee.

How much is a Costco membership?

Gold Star is $65 a year and Executive is $130. Check Costco for current pricing.

Do warehouse clubs price match?

No. Neither Costco nor Sam's Club matches competitors. Costco does refund its own price drops within 30 days.

Is a warehouse membership worth it for a small household?

It can be, if you buy non-perishables in bulk or split them with someone. Use the break-even math: fee divided by savings per trip.

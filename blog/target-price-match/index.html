Target Price Match Policy 2026: What Changed and How to Still Save

Target quietly ended competitor price matching in 2025. Here's what it still matches, how the 14-day window works, and how to make sure Target is really the cheapest.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

Target no longer matches competitors. Since July 28, 2025, Target only matches its own lower prices: a lower price in a Target store, on Target.com, in the Target app or through a Target Circle deal. You can ask at checkout or within 14 days after purchase. Amazon, Walmart and other retailers' prices are no longer matched.

What changed in Target's price match policy

For years, Target matched a list of competitors including Amazon, Walmart and Best Buy. That ended on July 28, 2025. Target now describes its policy as matching within its own ecosystem only, and it updated the rules again in January 2026, including how Target Circle deals work with price matches.

What Target still matches

  • Target.com and the Target app: if the online price is lower than the shelf, you can get the lower price.
  • Target's own price drops: if Target lowers the price within 14 days of your purchase, you can request the difference with your receipt.
  • Target Circle deals on the identical item.
  • Target Plus partner items sold on Target.com, when it's an exact match.

What Target won't match

  • Any outside retailer: Amazon (even items sold and shipped by Amazon), Walmart, Best Buy, Costco and others.
  • Clearance, damaged or refurbished items.
  • Target-specific offers such as Circle bonus rewards and registry completion coupons.
  • Some categories, such as alcohol and pharmacy purchases.

The item must be identical, and the lower price must be active and verifiable by Target. An expired sale or a screenshot usually won't be accepted.

How to get a Target price match or adjustment

  1. At checkout: open the Target app, find the item's lower Target.com price and show it to the cashier or Guest Services before paying.
  2. After you buy: within 14 days, bring your receipt (or order number) to Guest Services, or contact Target online if you bought on Target.com.
  3. Double-check the item: the size, model and color must match exactly.
Tip: Set a reminder for 14 days after a big Target purchase and check the price again. Price drops inside that window are one of the easiest refunds to claim.

FAQ

Does Target still price match Amazon?

No. Since July 28, 2025, Target doesn't match Amazon, even for items sold and shipped by Amazon.

Does Target price match Walmart?

No. Target no longer matches Walmart or any other outside retailer. It only matches its own lower prices.

How long do I have to get a Target price adjustment?

Within 14 days of purchase. If Target lowers its price on the identical item in that window, bring your receipt to Guest Services or contact Target for online orders.

Does Target match Target.com prices in store?

Yes. If Target.com or the Target app shows a lower price for the identical item, you can ask for that price in store.

Can I use Target Circle deals with a price match?

Target Circle deals are part of what Target matches, and Target updated the rules in January 2026. Some Target-specific offers, like Circle bonus rewards, are excluded, so check the details at Guest Services.

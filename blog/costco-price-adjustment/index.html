Costco Price Adjustment Policy: How to Get Money Back in 30 Days

Costco won't match other stores' prices, but it has one of the easiest price-drop refunds in retail. Here's how the 30-day rule works and how to claim it.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

Costco doesn't price match competitors like Walmart, Amazon or Sam's Club. What it offers is a price adjustment: if Costco lowers its own price on something you bought within 30 days, you can get the difference back. Warehouse purchases are adjusted at the membership or returns counter, and Costco.com orders through an online form.

How Costco's price adjustment works

Costco's policy is simple: if the price of an item you bought drops at Costco within 30 days of your purchase, Costco refunds the difference. A few details matter:

  • The clock starts on your purchase date, not when the item is delivered or installed.
  • Warehouse and online are separate. A warehouse purchase is compared with warehouse prices, and a Costco.com order with Costco.com prices. You can't claim a lower online price for something you bought in the warehouse, or the reverse.
  • The lower price usually has to still be active when you ask. Claim it while the sale is running.
  • Costco can decline any request at its discretion.

How to claim a Costco price adjustment

Warehouse purchases

  1. Note the item number from the shelf tag or the sale price in the Costco app.
  2. Go to the membership or returns counter with your membership card. A receipt helps, but Costco can usually look up the purchase from your membership.
  3. Get the difference refunded to your original payment method.

Costco.com orders

  1. Find your order number in your Costco.com account.
  2. Submit Costco's online price adjustment request with the order and item numbers, or contact customer service.
  3. Wait for the refund to your original payment method, typically within several business days.
Tip: If a request is declined but you're still within Costco's return window, some members return the item and buy it again at the lower price. Check your warehouse's rules before relying on this.

FAQ

Does Costco price match Walmart or Amazon?

No. Costco doesn't match any competitor, including Walmart, Sam's Club and Amazon. It only refunds the difference when Costco lowers its own price within 30 days.

How long do I have to get a Costco price adjustment?

30 days from the purchase date. For online orders, ask customer service if you're unsure how your order date is counted.

Do I need a receipt for a Costco price adjustment?

It helps, but for warehouse purchases Costco can usually find the transaction through your membership account.

Can I get the Costco.com price for something I bought in the warehouse?

No. Warehouse purchases are compared with warehouse prices and Costco.com orders with Costco.com prices.

What if the sale already ended?

Costco generally expects you to ask while the lower price is still active, so claim it as soon as you notice the drop.

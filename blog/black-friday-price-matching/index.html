Black Friday Price Matching 2026: Which Stores Match (and Which Don't)

Black Friday is the one week price matching mostly stops working. Here's what each store's rules say, the strategies that still save money, and how to tell a real deal from a fake one.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 9 min read
Quick answer

Most major retailers don't price match Black Friday deals. Best Buy blocks competitor matching from Thursday, Nov 19 through Monday, Nov 30, 2026. Walmart, Home Depot and Lowe's exclude Black Friday and special-event prices, and Target only matches its own prices. What still works: stores' own price-drop refunds after you buy, and comparing prices yourself before you check out.

5 strategies that still save money

1. Buy early, then claim the drop

If a store refunds its own price drops, buying during early sales is low-risk. If the price goes lower on Black Friday, claim the difference within the window. Target's holiday guarantee and Costco's 30-day adjustment work this way. Set a reminder so you don't miss it.

2. Know the real price before the sale starts

"Was $199, now $129" only means something if $199 was a real price. Scan the items on your list in late October or early November to see what they actually cost across stores. Then you'll recognize a genuine deal when it appears.

4. Watch for Black Friday-only models

Some TVs and electronics are made in special versions for the sale, with slightly different model numbers and specs. They're often hard to compare and never eligible for matching. Scan the barcode to see if the exact model is sold elsewhere.

5. Use Best Buy's normal matching after the blackout

If you're buying electronics and can wait, Best Buy's competitor matching returns after Cyber Monday. Early December prices on some items stay close to Black Friday levels.

How to spot a fake Black Friday deal

  • An inflated "was" price. If the item was never actually sold at the "original" price, the discount is fiction.
  • A different model. A familiar brand name at a surprising price can be a cheaper variant made for the sale.
  • Bundles you don't need. A bundle can look like a deal while costing more than buying the one item you want.
  • The same price as last month. Some "deals" are just the regular price with a red tag.
  • A cheaper price elsewhere. The easiest check of all: scan it and compare.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). Cyber Monday is November 30.

Does Best Buy price match on Black Friday?

No. Best Buy excludes competitor price matching from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after. In 2026 that is November 19 through November 30. Items advertised as Black Friday pricing are also excluded.

Does Walmart price match Black Friday deals?

No. Walmart doesn't match competitors at any time, and Black Friday and Cyber Monday prices on Walmart.com are excluded from its in-store Walmart.com price match.

Does Target price match during the holidays?

Target only matches its own lower prices. In 2025 it offered a Holiday Price Match Guarantee from November 1 to December 24 for Target price drops. Check whether it returns for 2026.

Should I buy early or wait for Black Friday?

If the store refunds its own price drops, buying early is low-risk: buy, then claim the difference if the price falls within the window. Know the item's normal price across stores so you can tell a real deal from a fake one.

Thanksgiving Dinner on a Budget: How to Save in 2026

Thanksgiving is on Thursday, November 26, 2026. Grocery stores compete hard for your holiday shopping, and that's a real opportunity if you plan for it.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

To host Thanksgiving on a budget, plan a simple menu early, buy your turkey during stores' holiday promotions (many grocers offer low per-pound prices or deals with a minimum purchase), stock up on pantry staples as they go on sale in early November, compare prices across stores for the big-ticket items, and plan for leftovers instead of over-buying.

Saving on the turkey

  • Watch the holiday promotions. Turkeys are a classic loss leader. Many grocers advertise low per-pound prices or deals with a minimum purchase. Compare the ads for your nearby stores.
  • Frozen is usually cheaper than fresh, and buying early is easy since it keeps.
  • Buy the right size. About 1–1.5 lbs per person covers dinner plus some leftovers.
  • Consider a turkey breast for a small group.

Saving on sides and desserts

  • Store brands for broth, canned vegetables, cranberry sauce, baking basics. See the easiest switches.
  • Make it from scratch where it's cheap and easy: mashed potatoes, stuffing, gravy.
  • Potluck it. Ask guests to bring a side or dessert.
  • Compare the big-ticket items across stores: the turkey, butter, cheese, coffee, wine or specialty items.

Plan for leftovers, not waste

Over-buying is the most common Thanksgiving budget mistake. Plan portions, and plan leftover meals (turkey soup, sandwiches, casseroles) so nothing goes to waste. Freeze extra turkey within a few days.

After dinner: Black Friday

Planning to shop the sales the next day? Most stores exclude Black Friday deals from price matching. See Black Friday price matching 2026 and how to spot a fake sale.

FAQ

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thursday, November 26, 2026.

How much turkey do I need per person?

About 1 to 1.5 pounds of whole turkey per person covers dinner plus some leftovers.

When should I buy a turkey to save money?

During grocery stores' holiday promotions in the weeks before Thanksgiving. Frozen turkeys keep, so you can buy when the price is lowest.

How long does a frozen turkey take to thaw?

In the refrigerator, allow about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds.

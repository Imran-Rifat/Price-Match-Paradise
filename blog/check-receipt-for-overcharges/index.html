How to Check Your Grocery Receipt for Overcharges (in 60 Seconds)

Register mistakes are easy to miss and easy to fix, if you catch them before you leave. Here's a fast receipt check, what to do when something's wrong, and how to find what you overpaid.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

To check your grocery receipt, look for items rung up twice, sale prices or coupons that didn't apply, wrong weights or produce codes, and multi-buy deals that didn't trigger. Check it before you leave the store and take any errors to customer service. A receipt scanner app can also show which items were cheaper at other stores.

The 60-second receipt check

  1. Watch the screen as items scan, especially sale items. It's the easiest time to catch a problem.
  2. Check the item count on the receipt against what's in your bags.
  3. Scan the prices of your sale items and big-ticket items. Those are where errors cost the most.
  4. Check the savings section for your coupons and member prices.
  5. Before you leave, take anything wrong to customer service with the receipt.

What to do if you were overcharged

  • Go to customer service with your receipt. If the shelf price was lower, you can often take a photo of the tag or walk an associate to it.
  • Ask about the store's scanning policy. Some retailers have voluntary policies that give you the item free or at a discount when it scans higher than the shelf price.
  • Know your state's rules. Some states have price-accuracy laws. Michigan's, for example, lets shoppers claim the difference plus a bonus. See why register prices don't match the shelf.

Habits that prevent overcharges

  • Clip digital coupons before you get to the register.
  • Scan your loyalty card or enter your number at the start of checkout.
  • Put sale items together on the belt so they're easier to watch.
  • Keep receipts (or use the store app) for at least two weeks for price-drop refunds.

FAQ

What should I check on my grocery receipt?

Look for double scans, sale prices or coupons that didn't apply, multi-buy deals that didn't trigger, wrong produce codes or weights, and missing member prices.

What do I do if I was overcharged at the grocery store?

Take your receipt to customer service, ideally before you leave. If the shelf price was lower, show the tag. Many stores fix it on the spot.

Do stores give you the item free if it scans wrong?

Some retailers have voluntary scanning policies that do, and a few states have price-accuracy laws. Ask customer service about the store's policy.

How can I tell if I paid more than another store?

Scan your receipt with a receipt scanner app that compares each item with prices at nearby stores.

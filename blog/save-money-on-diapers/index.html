How to Save Money on Diapers and Baby Essentials

Diapers and wipes are one of the biggest ongoing costs of a new baby, and prices for the exact same box vary a lot. Here's how to pay less without cutting corners.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

To save on diapers, compare the cost per diaper instead of the box price, try store brands, buy bigger boxes only when your baby won't outgrow the size soon, stock up during sales, and compare prices across stores for the exact same box. The identical product often costs noticeably different amounts at different stores.

8 ways to spend less

  1. Compare stores for the exact box. The same UPC can differ by several dollars between nearby stores.
  2. Try the store brand. Many families find store-brand diapers and wipes work just as well for their baby. Try a small pack first. See store brand vs. name brand.
  3. Don't over-buy one size. Babies can move up a size quickly. Buy big boxes of the current size only when you're sure they'll be used.
  4. Stock up at the sale-cycle low, and stack store coupons and manufacturer coupons. See coupon stacking.
  5. Use loyalty rewards at the store you buy diapers from most. See are loyalty programs worth it.
  6. Consider subscriptions carefully. Subscribe-and-save discounts can help, but compare the final price with stores.
  7. Buy wipes in bulk, since they don't change size.
  8. Check warehouse clubs, but compare per diaper. See when bulk is worth it.

A note on formula

Formula is another identical-everywhere product where prices vary between stores, so comparing the exact container can save money. Don't switch formula types without talking to your pediatrician, and never dilute formula to make it last longer.

Other baby essentials

Baby shampoo, lotion, rash cream and bottles are all barcoded, identical products, so compare them the same way. Price matching can help too. See which stores still match.

FAQ

How do I compare diaper prices?

Divide the box price by the number of diapers to get the cost per diaper, then compare the same box across stores.

Are store-brand diapers worth it?

Many families find they work well and cost less. Try a small pack first to see how they fit your baby.

Should I buy diapers in bulk?

Buy bulk boxes of the current size when your baby won't outgrow it soon. Wipes are a safer bulk buy since they don't change size.

Where are diapers cheapest?

It varies by store and week. Compare the exact box across nearby stores, including warehouse clubs, and watch for sales and coupons.

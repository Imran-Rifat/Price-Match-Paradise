The model-number traps

  • Retailer-specific TV models. The same TV line often has different model numbers per retailer, sometimes with different panels, stands or remotes. Those won't match.
  • Laptop configurations. Same screen size and name, different RAM, storage or processor. Compare the full model number.
  • Bundles. A bundle with a mount or software is a different item.
  • Open-box and refurbished are excluded from matching.
  • Carrier phone deals tied to activation or trade-in generally don't qualify.

FAQ

Does Best Buy price match TVs?

Yes, for identical, in-stock TVs sold by one of its 19 listed competitors. The model number must match exactly, and competitor matching pauses during Thanksgiving week.

Why do TVs have different model numbers at different stores?

Manufacturers often make retailer-specific versions that can differ in panel, stand, remote or features. Stores only match the identical model.

Can I price match a laptop?

Yes, at stores that match competitors, but the configuration must be identical: same processor, RAM and storage, not just the same model family.

Can I price match a phone?

Unlocked phones sold by a listed retailer can qualify. Carrier deals tied to activation or trade-in generally don't.

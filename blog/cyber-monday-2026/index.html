The 5-point Cyber Monday deal check

  1. Compare other stores now. Is the "deal" lower than the regular price elsewhere?
  2. Confirm the exact model. Special versions made for the sale often have different model numbers.
  3. Ignore "compare at" and inflated "was" prices. See 9 signs of a fake sale.
  4. Add shipping and fees to get the real total.
  5. Check the seller. Marketplace sellers can have different return policies.

Protections that still work

  • Price drops after you buy: some stores refund their own price drops within set windows. Target ran a holiday guarantee from Nov 1 to Dec 24 in 2025. See stores that refund price drops.
  • Holiday return windows: many retailers extend returns for holiday purchases. Check each store's terms.
  • Matching resumes after the blackout: Best Buy's competitor matching returns after Cyber Monday.

A simple plan

  1. Make a list before November and note normal prices across stores.
  2. Set price-drop alerts on the items you want.
  3. On the day, check each deal against your notes and other stores.
  4. Buy only what's on the list.

FAQ

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Monday, November 30, 2026, the Monday after Thanksgiving.

Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday?

It depends on the item. The sales now overlap heavily. Compare the price with other stores whichever day you shop.

Do stores price match Cyber Monday deals?

Mostly no. Many stores exclude holiday sale prices, and Best Buy pauses competitor matching from Nov 19 through Nov 30 in 2026.

How can I tell if a Cyber Monday deal is real?

Compare it with the current price at other stores, confirm the exact model, ignore "compare at" prices, and include shipping.

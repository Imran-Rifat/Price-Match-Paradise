How to Ask for a Price Match: What to Say (with Scripts)

Asking for a price match feels awkward the first time. It shouldn't. With the right proof ready and a short, friendly ask, it takes less than a minute.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 5 min read
Quick answer

To ask for a price match, have the competitor's live price for the identical item on your phone, tell the cashier before they finish ringing you up, and keep it short: "Hi, I have a price match on this item. [Store] has it for [price]. Here's the listing." Stay friendly. If the cashier can't do it, ask for customer service.

Before you ask: have these ready

  1. The store matches competitors. Check first. See which stores still do.
  2. A live listing showing the competitor's name, the exact item and the price.
  3. The identical item: same brand, size and model. The barcode is the easiest proof.
  4. Stock status showing the competitor has it available.

Scripts that work

At the register

"Hi! Before you ring this one up, I have a price match. [Store] has the same item for [price]. Here's the listing."

At customer service

"Hi, I'd like to price match this. It's the same model at [Store] for [price], sold by [Store] and in stock. Here's the page."

Online chat or phone (before ordering)

"Hi, I'd like to order [item, model number] and request a price match. [Store] has it for [price], sold and shipped by [Store], in stock. Here's the link: [URL]."

For a price adjustment after you bought it

"Hi, I bought this on [date] and the price has dropped to [price]. Could you adjust it? Here's my receipt."

If the answer is no

  • Ask what the issue is. It's often fixable, like a different size or a marketplace seller.
  • Ask for a manager politely. They usually have more discretion.

Small things that help

  • Use a staffed register. Self-checkout can't override prices.
  • Ask early in the transaction, before everything is rung up.
  • Avoid the rush. A busy Saturday line isn't the best time for a first try.
  • Keep your phone unlocked and the page open. Fumbling slows everyone down.

FAQ

What do I say to get a price match?

Keep it short: "I have a price match on this item. [Store] has it for [price]. Here's the listing." Show the live listing on your phone.

When should I ask for a price match?

Before the cashier finishes ringing you up, or at customer service before you pay.

What do I need to show?

A live listing with the competitor's name, the identical item, the price and stock status.

Is it rude to ask for a price match?

No. Price matching is a published store policy. A friendly, prepared request is completely normal.

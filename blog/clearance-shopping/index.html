Clearance Shopping: How to Find Markdowns Worth Buying

Clearance can be the cheapest shelf in the store or a trap that gets you to buy things you don't need. Here's how to find the real markdowns and check them fast.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 6 min read
Quick answer

To find real clearance deals, learn where your store puts markdowns (often end caps, a back-corner section, or the bottom shelf of the aisle), check the tag for the markdown pattern, shop seasonal clearance right after a holiday or season ends, and compare the clearance price with other stores before you buy.

Where markdowns hide

  • A dedicated clearance section, often in a back corner or along a side wall.
  • The bottom shelf of the regular aisle, where the item normally lives.
  • End caps at the ends of aisles.
  • Near the department for seasonal goods, like garden or holiday items.
  • Manager's special stickers on meat, bakery and dairy near their sell-by date.

How to read a clearance tag

Many chains use consistent tag colors or price endings to signal how deep a markdown is and whether more cuts are coming. Patterns vary by retailer, and staff often know them. It's worth asking when markdowns happen in that department. See when stores mark down.

Clearance rules to remember

  • Usually excluded from price matching and often from price adjustments.
  • Check the return policy. Final-sale items can't be returned.
  • Check dates on food, and inspect for damage.
  • Only buy what you'd have bought anyway. See 9 signs of a fake sale.

FAQ

How do I find clearance items in a store?

Look for a dedicated clearance section, the bottom shelf of the regular aisle, end caps, and manager's special stickers on perishables.

When is the best time to shop clearance?

Right after a holiday or the end of a season, in January for holiday overstock, and when packaging changes.

Can you price match clearance items?

Usually not. Most price match policies exclude clearance, closeout and final-sale items.

Is clearance always the lowest price?

No. Clearance is a markdown from that store's price. Another store's regular price can still be lower, so compare before you buy.

What's the Best Day to Go Grocery Shopping? (What Actually Matters)

You'll see lots of confident answers online. The honest one: it depends on your store. Here's how to find the best day and time for yours, and what matters more than timing.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 6 min read
Quick answer

There's no single best day for every store. The best time to shop is usually when your store's new weekly ad starts (often midweek or Sunday, depending on the chain), when markdowns go out (ask the meat or bakery department), and when the store is quiet, often weekday mornings. Comparing prices across stores usually saves more than picking the right day.

1. When the weekly ad changes

Many chains start new weekly ads midweek, and others on Sunday. Check your store's app or website to see when yours starts. The first day of a new ad means fresh sale prices and full stock on advertised items. Some stores have an overlap day where both the old and new ads are valid.

2. When markdowns happen

Stores mark down meat, bakery, deli and produce that's nearing its sell-by date. The timing varies by store and department, often early in the day. The easiest way to find out is to ask the department staff when they usually mark items down.

Tip: Marked-down meat is a great deal if you cook it that day or freeze it right away.

3. When the store is quiet

Weekday mornings and later evenings are often less crowded than weekend afternoons. A quieter store means less rushing, fewer impulse buys and more time to check prices and unit prices. Store apps and map apps often show busy times.

4. When shelves are stocked

If items you need often sell out, ask when the store gets deliveries or restocks that department. It's usually a consistent schedule.

FAQ

What is the cheapest day to go grocery shopping?

There's no universal cheapest day. The first day of your store's weekly ad, and times when markdowns go out, are often good. Ask your store when both happen.

What day do grocery stores mark down meat?

It varies by store and department. Ask the meat department when they usually mark down items. It's often early in the day.

When is the grocery store least busy?

Often weekday mornings and later evenings. Store apps and map apps can show busy times for your location.

Does shopping on a certain day really save money?

A little, through fresh sales and markdowns. Comparing prices across stores usually saves more.

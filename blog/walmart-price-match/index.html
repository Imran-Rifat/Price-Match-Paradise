Does Walmart Price Match? The 2026 Policy, Explained

Short answer: only its own website. Here's exactly what Walmart will match, what it won't, and how to make sure you're still paying the lowest price.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 7 min read
Quick answer

Walmart price matches only Walmart.com. In a US Walmart store, if the identical item is cheaper and in stock on Walmart.com, you can ask for that price at checkout. Walmart does not match competitors like Amazon, Target or Aldi, and it doesn't refund the difference if a price drops after you buy.

How Walmart's price match works in 2026

Walmart used to match competitor ads, but its US policy is now narrow. According to Walmart's published policy, a US store will match the price of an identical item advertised on Walmart.com when:

  • The item is exactly the same: size, model, quantity, brand and color.
  • It's in stock and available to buy on Walmart.com at the moment you ask.
  • It's sold by Walmart itself, not a Marketplace seller.
  • You tell an associate at checkout, before you pay.

Quantities are limited to one per customer, per item, per day. The policy doesn't apply in Alaska, Hawaii or Puerto Rico, and the store manager has the final say.

Watch out for Rollbacks. Rollback prices on Walmart.com are excluded under the special-event rule. If the cheaper online price is a Rollback, expect a no.

How to ask for a Walmart price match (step by step)

  1. Open Walmart.com or the Walmart app on your phone and find the exact item, sold by Walmart.
  2. Check it's in stock online and that the listing isn't a Rollback or clearance event.
  3. Tell the cashier before they finish ringing you up, or go to customer service first. Self-checkout can't override prices, so ask an associate.
  4. Show the live listing and point out the matching size and model.

Once you've paid, there's no published adjustment window to fall back on, so ask before you pay.

FAQ

Does Walmart price match Amazon?

No. Walmart's US policy excludes competitor prices, including Amazon. It only matches Walmart.com prices for identical items sold by Walmart.

Does Walmart price match Target?

No. Walmart doesn't match Target or any other retailer. Target also stopped matching competitors in July 2025.

Does Walmart price match its own website?

Yes. In US stores, Walmart matches the Walmart.com price of an identical item that is sold by Walmart and in stock online, if you ask at checkout. Rollbacks, clearance and other limited-time events on Walmart.com are excluded.

Will Walmart refund the difference if the price drops after I buy?

No. Walmart's published policy doesn't offer price adjustments on items that drop in price after purchase, online or in store. Ask for any match before you pay.

Can I price match at Walmart self-checkout?

Not on your own. Price overrides need an associate, so use a staffed register or customer service.

Does Walmart Canada price match?

Walmart Canada ended its competitor Ad Match program in 2020. It now matches Walmart.ca prices for identical in-stock items purchased in store.

Home Depot vs. Lowe's Price Match: How They Compare in 2026

Both home improvement giants still match competitor prices, and they match each other. Here's what qualifies, what's excluded, and how to get the lower price.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 8 min read
Quick answer

Yes, both Home Depot and Lowe's price match. Each will match a competitor's current lower price on an identical, in-stock item, including each other, Walmart, Target and Amazon (items sold by Amazon itself). Neither beats the price by an extra 10% anymore. It's a straight match. Clearance, special buys, bundles and marketplace sellers are excluded.

About the old "beat by 10%": You'll still see older articles promising 10% off the competitor's price. That's out of date. Home Depot reportedly ended its extra 10% in February 2026, and Lowe's current published policy is a straight match.

Home Depot's price match rules

  • Identical item: same brand, model number, color, size and specifications.
  • In stock at both Home Depot and the competitor.
  • Proof: a current online listing or ad on your phone.
  • Competitors: local stores and major online retailers such as Lowe's, Walmart, Target and Amazon (sold by Amazon). Warehouse clubs are excluded.
  • Excluded: clearance and closeouts, Special Buys and limited-quantity deals, Black Friday and similar events, bundles, open-box and damaged items, installation and labor.
  • After you buy: Home Depot typically allows a price adjustment within 30 days if the item gets cheaper at Home Depot or at a qualifying competitor. Confirm the window at the service desk.

Lowe's price match rules

  • Identical item: same model number, color, size and included accessories.
  • In stock and available for delivery to your area.
  • Shipping counts: Lowe's compares the competitor's total price, including delivery.
  • Competitors: local and online retailers such as Home Depot, Menards, Ace Hardware, Walmart, Target, Best Buy and Amazon (sold by Amazon). Membership clubs are excluded.
  • Excluded: clearance, seasonal items, Black Friday deals, BOGO and bundle offers, and marketplace sellers. Lowe's coupons and discounts generally can't be combined with a match.
  • After you buy: Lowe's accepts requests after purchase but doesn't publish a fixed window, so ask as soon as you spot a lower price.

How to get the match (either store)

  1. Scan the item's barcode to find the lowest price on that exact model and confirm the competitor has it in stock.
  2. Go to a register or the service desk and show the live listing on your phone.
  3. Buying online? Use chat or call customer service with a link to the competitor's listing before you check out.

FAQ

Does Home Depot price match Lowe's?

Yes. Home Depot matches Lowe's current price on an identical, in-stock item, and Lowe's matches Home Depot the same way.

Does Lowe's still beat competitor prices by 10%?

No. Lowe's current policy is a straight price match. Older articles mentioning an extra 10% off are out of date.

Does Home Depot price match Amazon?

Yes, for items sold and shipped by Amazon itself. Third-party marketplace sellers on Amazon don't qualify.

Do Home Depot and Lowe's match Costco?

No. Both exclude membership warehouse clubs such as Costco and Sam's Club.

Can I get a price match after buying at Home Depot?

Typically yes, within 30 days, if the item gets cheaper at Home Depot or a qualifying competitor. Bring your receipt and confirm at the service desk.

Which Stores Price Match Amazon in 2026?

Want Amazon's price but need the item today? A few big stores will still match it, but only if you know the rules. Here's who does, who doesn't, and how to ask.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 6 min read
Quick answer

Best Buy, Home Depot and Lowe's still price match Amazon in 2026, as long as the item is sold and shipped by Amazon itself (not a third-party seller), identical, and in stock. Target stopped matching Amazon in July 2025, and Walmart and Costco don't match competitors at all. Amazon itself doesn't price match.

The rules that trip people up

  • "Sold by Amazon" only. On the product page, check "Ships from" and "Sold by." If a third-party seller is listed, stores won't match it.
  • Prime-only prices don't count. Membership-exclusive deals, Lightning Deals and coupon prices are usually excluded.
  • Identical model. Same brand, model number, size and color. Check the barcode or model number, not the product name.
  • In stock now. If Amazon shows "currently unavailable" or a long delivery date, expect a no.
  • Shipping may count. Lowe's compares the delivered price, so a cheap item with expensive shipping may not be cheaper.

FAQ

Does Best Buy price match Amazon?

Yes, for items sold and shipped by Amazon that are identical and in stock. Prime-exclusive prices are excluded, and competitor matching is paused from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after.

Does Target price match Amazon?

No. Target stopped matching Amazon and all other competitors on July 28, 2025.

Does Walmart price match Amazon?

No. Walmart only matches Walmart.com prices in its US stores.

Does Home Depot price match Amazon?

Yes, for identical items sold and shipped by Amazon that are in stock. It's a straight match with no extra discount.

Does Amazon price match other stores?

No. Amazon doesn't match other retailers' prices.

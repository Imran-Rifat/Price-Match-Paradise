Barcode Won't Scan? 9 Quick Fixes for Your Phone

Most scanning problems come down to light, distance or the wrong code. Here's how to fix them in seconds, and what to do when a code simply won't work.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 6 min read
Quick answer

If a barcode won't scan, fix the light first (avoid glare, add light), hold the phone 4–8 inches away with the whole barcode in frame, keep it steady, and tilt curved or shiny packages slightly. Make sure you're scanning the product's barcode, not a shelf tag or store label. If it still fails, type the number printed under the barcode.

9 fixes that solve most scanning problems

  1. Kill the glare. Shiny wrappers and cans reflect overhead lights. Tilt the package slightly.
  2. Add light. In a dim aisle, use your phone's flashlight, or move to better light.
  3. Back up a little. About 4–8 inches away works best. Too close and the camera can't focus.
  4. Get the whole code in frame, including the quiet space on both sides of the bars.
  5. Hold steady for a second, and brace your elbow if needed.
  6. Flatten or rotate curved packages. Turn cans so the bars run vertically along the curve.
  7. Clean your camera lens. A smudged lens is a surprisingly common cause.
  8. Try the other barcode. Multipacks often have an outer barcode and individual barcodes. Scan the one for what you're buying.
  9. Type it in. Enter the digits printed under the barcode manually.

Check camera permission

If the scanner shows a black screen, the app may not have camera access. On iPhone, go to Settings, find the app and turn on Camera. On Android, go to Settings, Apps, the app, Permissions, Camera. Picking a good scanner app matters too. See what to look for.

FAQ

Why won't a barcode scan with my phone?

The most common causes are glare, low light, holding the phone too close, a smudged lens, or scanning a store label instead of the product's barcode.

How far should I hold my phone from a barcode?

About 4–8 inches, with the whole barcode and a little space around it in the frame.

Can I type in a barcode number instead?

Yes. The digits printed under the barcode are the same code, and most scanner apps let you enter them manually.

Why can't I scan deli or produce labels for price comparison?

Store-printed weight labels and produce PLU stickers aren't product-specific barcodes, so they can't be compared across stores.

Best Buy Price Match Guarantee: The Full 2026 Guide

Best Buy is one of the few big retailers that still matches competitors, including Amazon. Here's who qualifies, what's excluded, and how to get approved the first time.

Quick answer

Yes, Best Buy price matches. Its Price Match Guarantee matches the current price of an identical, in-stock item sold directly by 19 named retailers, including Amazon, Walmart, Target, Costco, Home Depot and Apple. Marketplace sellers, membership-only prices, open-box items and the Thanksgiving week sales are excluded.

The item must be sold and shipped by that retailer. A third-party seller on Amazon or Walmart.com doesn't count.

What qualifies (and what doesn't)

To qualify

  • Identical product: same brand, model number and specifications, and new.
  • In stock at the competitor when you ask.
  • Current price that Best Buy can verify on the competitor's site.

Excluded

  • Marketplace or third-party sellers, including on BestBuy.com.
  • Membership-only prices such as Prime-exclusive deals.
  • Coupons, promo codes, bundles and "free item" offers.
  • Open-box, refurbished, used and clearance items.
  • Out-of-stock competitor items.
Thanksgiving blackout: Best Buy doesn't match competitors from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after, or items advertised as Black Friday pricing. Plan big holiday purchases around it.

The model-number trap (and how to avoid it)

The #1 reason electronics price matches get denied is a slightly different model. TVs, laptops and appliances often come in retailer-specific versions with different suffixes, and Best Buy only matches the identical model.

How to request a Best Buy price match

  1. In store: bring the item (or its details) to checkout or customer service and show the competitor's live product page on your phone.
  2. Online: contact Best Buy through chat or by phone with a link to the competitor's listing before you order.
  3. The associate verifies that it's the same model, in stock and sold by the competitor, then adjusts the price.

Price drops after you buy

Best Buy can also refund the difference after you buy. For most products and most customers the window is 15 days, with a shorter window for some activatable devices like phones. My Best Buy Plus and Total members get a longer window, up to 60 days on most eligible products, for Best Buy's own price drops.

Tip: After a big purchase, scan it again a week later. If the price has dropped anywhere on the list, you still have time to ask for the difference.

FAQ

Does Best Buy price match Amazon?

Yes, as long as the item is sold and shipped by Amazon (not a third-party seller), is identical and in stock, and isn't a Prime-exclusive price.

Does Best Buy price match Walmart and Target?

Yes. Both are on Best Buy's list of qualified competitors. The item must be sold by Walmart or Target directly and be in stock.

Does Best Buy price match during Black Friday?

No. Competitor price matching is blacked out from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after, and items advertised as Black Friday pricing are excluded.

Can I get a price match after buying at Best Buy?

Generally yes, within the return and exchange period: 15 days for most products for most customers, and longer for My Best Buy Plus and Total members on Best Buy's own price drops.

Does Best Buy price match open-box items?

No. Open-box, refurbished, used and clearance items are excluded from the Price Match Guarantee.

Why was my Best Buy price match denied?

The most common reasons are a different model number, a third-party seller, an out-of-stock competitor item, or a membership-only or coupon price. Comparing by barcode helps make sure the model is identical.

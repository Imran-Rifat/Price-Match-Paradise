Barcode Lookup: How to Find a Product by Its UPC Number

Have the number but not the product? A UPC lookup can identify exactly what it is, and more usefully, what it costs at stores near you.

Price Match Paradise Team Updated September 22, 2026 6 min read
Quick answer

To look up a product by barcode, enter the 12-digit UPC (or 13-digit EAN) printed under the bars into a barcode lookup or price comparison app. It identifies the exact brand, product and size, and a price comparison app also shows what that item costs at stores near you.

What a barcode lookup shows

  • The exact product: brand, item, size and variant.
  • Product details like packaging and category, depending on the database.
  • Prices, if you use a price comparison app rather than a plain lookup tool.

A plain lookup tells you what something is. A price comparison scanner tells you where to buy it cheapest, which is usually the reason you're looking.

How to look up a UPC

  1. Find the number printed under the barcode, usually 12 digits (UPC) or 13 (EAN).
  2. Type it exactly, including any leading zero.
  3. Check the result matches the package: same brand, size and variant.

When a barcode lookup is handy

  • Checking a price before you buy, without hunting through search results.
  • Confirming two items are identical for a price match.
  • Re-ordering the exact product you bought last time.
  • Identifying an unlabeled item from a photo of its barcode.

FAQ

How do I look up a product by barcode?

Enter the 12-digit UPC or 13-digit EAN printed under the barcode into a lookup or price comparison app. It identifies the exact product, and a price app also shows local prices.

Can I search by UPC number instead of scanning?

Yes. The number under the barcode is the same code the scanner reads, so typing it works when a scan fails.

Why does my barcode lookup return no results?

Common causes are a typo, a missing leading zero, a new or regional product, or a store-printed label that isn't a product code.

About

An independent tool for comparing the product in front of you.

Price Match Paradise is a barcode price-comparison and shopping-route app developed by Imran Hossan Rifat. The Android listing is published under Save Depot; both names refer to the same app and developer.

What the app does

Shoppers can scan a product barcode or search by name, review available retailer offers, consult price-match information, build a shopping list and estimate whether a multi-store route remains worthwhile after fuel cost.

Independent status

Price Match Paradise is an independent shopping tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the retailers whose public offers or policies may appear in results. Retailer names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

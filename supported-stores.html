Complete retailer directory

137 supported retailer sources

The current Price Match Paradise dataset contains 58 Canadian and 79 U.S. retailer sources—including Rona, Save-On-Foods, No Frills, FreshCo, Kroger, Walmart, Target and many more. A supported source is one the app can use for product discovery or price comparison; it does not mean every product is available or that the retailer will approve a price match.

Dataset reviewed October 1, 2026

Important: Always open or verify the retailer offer before traveling or requesting a price match. Prices, stock, shipping, membership requirements, seller identity, and store policies can change.

What “supported” does and does not mean

Supported meansIt does not guarantee

How to verify a result

  1. Confirm the brand, model, size, colour and UPC or model number.
  2. Open the retailer page and check that the offer is current and available.
  3. Check seller, shipping, membership and location conditions.
  4. Read the retailer's current price-match policy before asking for a match.

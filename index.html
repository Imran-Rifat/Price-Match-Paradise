Why shoppers keep coming back

Three tools. One mission: keep more money in your pocket.

A barcode scanner that finds the cheapest store, a receipt scanner that shows what you overpaid, and a savings tracker that watches it add up — all in one app.

In-aisle barcode scanner

Point your camera at a product barcode and review available matching offers across supported retailers.

  • Live prices refreshed on every scan
  • Show-screen proof for price-match requests
  • Works on groceries, pharmacy, baby care & more

Smart receipt scanner

Photograph any receipt after shopping. The app identifies every line item, finds what you overpaid, and shows the cheaper alternative nearby.

  • Total overpayment calculated per trip
  • Suggests the best store to switch to next time
  • Builds a monthly savings history you can track

Your savings dashboard

Every scan and receipt feeds a running total of what you've saved, with price-drop alerts for the items you buy most often.

  • Total savings tracked over time
  • Price-drop alerts for items you buy regularly
  • See your best and worst stores at a glance

How it works

Scan. Compare. Save. Repeat every week.

1

Scan

Point your camera at a product barcode, or search by product name when scanning is not available.

2

Compare

Review available offers for the matching product across supported retailers, ranked by listed price.

3

Save

4

Track

Scan receipts to see what you overpaid. Watch your monthly savings grow with every trip.
